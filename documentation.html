Design options

Four homepage designs in two families. Coastal is calm and serif-led in navy and seafoam. Sunny Shoreline is warmer and bolder in teal, sand and coral.

The three alternates are homepage designs. Each has its own stylesheet and script, and their menu links open the shared inner pages, which use the Coastal A1 design.

Page by page

Where each original page went, and what is different on its replacement.

Original pageRedesigned pageWhat changed
Home/ Homeindex.html The photo slider, welcome paragraph and sidebar are replaced by a single-column page: headline with two actions, four highlights, a pets section, amenities with nearby dining and shopping, two guest reviews and a closing booking call.
Vacation Rentals/vacation-rentals/ Vacation Rentalsvacation-rentals.html The same Vacation RentPro booking engine, embedded at full width. A one-line introduction is added above it, and a line with the phone number and a Rental Policies link below.
Photo Gallery/photo-gallery/ Not carried over The original page holds four photos and three short captions. The redesign has no gallery page.
Local Attractions/local-attractions/ Things to Dothings-to-do.html The golf, restaurant and boat rental links are kept as link cards, with Harry T's moved to the pet-friendly list. New: pet-friendly parks, shopping, boat rentals and restaurants with descriptions, three event calendars, and a link to Visit Florida.
News and Events/category/news-and-events/ Not carried over Its two posts are short guest quotes. Both now appear in the reviews section of the homepage.
About Us/about-us/ Aboutabout-us.html The same wording, owners' photo and listings button, in the new page layout with a booking card alongside.
Rental Policies/rental-policies/ Rental Policiesrental-policies.html All 35 items are kept word for word. They are split into two headed sections, Agreement Terms and Conditions and Condo Association Rules, as a numbered list with bullet lists for the pet, pool and beach rules.
Guest Reviews/guest-reviews/ Reviewsguest-reviews.html The published review is carried over, with an average rating summary above it. The plugin's form is replaced by a Write a Review form with the same fields, which sends the review by email for approval.
FAQs/faqs/ FAQsfaqs.html The original page holds placeholder text. The redesign has seven questions in an expandable list. The answers are sample copy awaiting confirmation.
Airlines and Car Rentals/airlines-and-car-rentals/ Getting Heregetting-here.html The three airport links are kept. New: a short introduction to the area, a map, and a car rental or shuttle link for each airport.
New Pet Infopet-info.html The pet policy and pet fines, taken from the Rental Policies, plus pet-friendly things to do around Destin.

Main design

Coastal A1

index.html · styles.css · site.js

Airy and centered. The headline sits above a wide photo, section bands alternate between white sand, seafoam and pale sky, and soft wave dividers separate them. Buttons have 12px corners. This design is used by every inner page.

Logo

  • Mark: a paw print above two shoreline waves. Paw in Deep Navy #17324D, waves in #5E9C90.
  • Wordmark: "Destin Pet Friendly Condos" in Newsreader Medium (500), 21px in the header, Deep Navy.
  • On navy: white paw and wordmark with waves in #9FD3C7.
  • Site icon: the paw over a single wave on a navy tile, simplified so it stays clear at 16 pixels (favicon.svg, favicon.ico, apple-touch-icon.png).

Alternate design

Sunny Shoreline B2

index-b2.html · styles-b2.css · site-b2.js

The boldest of the four. Extra-bold headlines sit beside a photo with a curved edge and a trail of paw prints, buttons are pills, and a house-and-paw logo replaces the plain paw. The footer is deep teal.

Logo

  • Mark: a house outline with a paw print inside. Outline in Teal #0D5C63, paw in Driftwood #D9A15F. 36px in the header.
  • Wordmark: "Destin Pet Friendly Condos" on two lines in Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold (800), 16px in the header, Teal.
  • Footer: white outline and wordmark with the paw in Driftwood Light #E8B97E, on Teal Deep. The mark is 44px and the wordmark 21px.
  • Rule: Driftwood is for the logo paw and the paw-print trail only, never for text.
