Pet Policy

  • PETS: The keeping of a dog, cat, or other animal is not a right of an owner/guest but is a conditional license. This is subject to immediate termination at any time upon finding that a pet is vicious, annoying to other residents, or has in any way become a nuisance. The right to have a pet is subject to the following conditions:
    • All pets must always be on a leash and under the control of a responsible party.
    • Pet must not be curbed or tied any place on the Maravilla grounds or unit balconies.
    • Pets must not be left unattended on a unit balcony and are never to be left unattended in a public place.
    • A pet walk area has been established on the grounds at the North end of Building 1.
    • All owners of pets shall be held strictly responsible to immediately collect and properly dispose of the wastes and litter of their pets.
    • Pets should be kept away from homeowners’ properties.
    • All pet owners shall have a plastic bag when walking their pets outside.
    • Pets are not allowed on the beach unless they have a Walton County license and adhere to Walton County pet/beach rules.
  • PET FINES PER VIOLATION: Pet fines $200 fine/violation

Things to Do With Your Pet

Parks and Outdoor Spaces

  • Destin Dog Park (opens in a new tab)

    Located at the Morgan Sports Center, this 3-acre fenced park features separate off-leash play areas for large and small dogs, shade trees, and water stations.

  • Henderson Beach State Park (opens in a new tab)

    Pets are welcome on the park's scenic nature trails and picnic areas (note that pets are not allowed on the actual state park beach).

  • Scenic Gulf Drive (opens in a new tab)

    A great paved walkway for exercising your pet while taking in coastal views near the beach.

Shopping and Strolling

  • Destin Commons (opens in a new tab)

    This open-air shopping center welcomes leashed dogs throughout the common areas, with pet hydration stations and select pet-friendly indoor stores.

  • HarborWalk Village & Destin Harbor Boardwalk (opens in a new tab)

    A lively place to walk your dog on a leash past boats, shops, and outdoor areas.

  • The Village of Baytowne Wharf (opens in a new tab)

    Located nearby in Miramar Beach, this open-air village allows leashed pets in outdoor walking areas.

On the Water & Boat Rentals

  • Crab Island (opens in a new tab)

    Many visitors bring their dogs along on private boat rentals, pontoon charters, or private tours to anchor at the famous sandbar. Local companies like Endless Weekend Pontoons LLC offer pet-friendly boat options.

  • Wet-N-Wild Watersports (opens in a new tab)

    Wet N Wild Watersports in Destin, FL, offers dog-friendly pontoon boat rentals, perfect for exploring the Gulf Coast with your pup.

Restaurants and Patios

  • Boshamps Seafood and Oyster House (opens in a new tab)

    A popular harbor-front restaurant where dogs are welcome on the outdoor patio (the restaurant's mascot is even a dog).

  • Harry T's Lighthouse (opens in a new tab)

    Offers waterfront outdoor seating on the harbor that accommodates pets.

  • Dewey Destin's Harborside (opens in a new tab)

    A casual, pet-friendly spot right by the water for seafood.
