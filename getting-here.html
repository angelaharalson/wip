Getting Here

Between the city of Destin and Fort Walton Beach, there are 24 miles of white-sand beaches that are blessed by Gulf of Mexico surf that transitions in color from pale green glass to emerald, and all of it is backed by blue sky.

Destin is 50 miles east of Pensacola and 150 miles west of Florida's capital, Tallahassee.
